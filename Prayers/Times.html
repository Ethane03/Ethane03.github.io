Occasional Prayers

In a time of disease and plague

O Almighty God, who though You brought plague upon Your own people in wrath, because they oppposed Moses and Aaron in the wilderness, still had mercy when they called upon you, and released the plague, may you now remove the rod of your wrath from us sinners, who are infected by a great disease. Spread over us the wings of your mercy. Grant us forgiveness of sins and compassion. Punish us not as you did Egypt. Put us not to shame as you did Babylon. Punish not this land on account of our sins. O Lord, stretch out your holy hand and bless us, your people. O Compassionate One, we knock at your door. May you remove this disease, this harsh illness from us and make haste to save us, now and always and forever. Amen.

In Sorrow and Affliction

Merciful God, help us to know and believe that you must send us sorrows and trials in order to return us to you for our forgiveness. Lead us with a merciful gaze and do not discipline us according to our sins. Grant us grace to patiently suffer the sorrows you send to us, and to find refuge beneath your hand and staff. Treat us kindly, O Lord, for you know well our weaknesses. Wipe away our tears and make us glad. Amen.

In War and Conflict

O God, all powerful, the true ruler of all the nations of the earth, omnipotent and just judge, keep us from the hand of our enemies. Show them their injustice and pride. Make their plans fail. Humble them in your presence, and unite their souls to your will. Make these days suitable for the glory of the Holy Name, and the health of humankind. May the omnipotence and monarchy of your goodness shine throughout the world. Send justice and peace among all the peoples of the world. Grant us the grace to wear your salvation as armor, ever redeemed from all afflictions, and praise you who grants every victory. Amen.

For Rain

O Lord, who conducts all things without error, we humbly beseech you to have mercy on us, and send showers of rain, so that our harvests might be plentiful, and we might partake of the fruit of the earth, for our needs and for your glory. Amen.

For Rain to end

O Lord, full of great grace and righteousness, may you grant us bright days, so that we might harvest the fruits of the earth in their time. For though we have become worthy of watery affliction by our transgressions, we truly repent. Make your servants worthy to correct our footsteps and praise you for your blessings. Amen.

In Trials

O Lord our Messiah, who was tested like us in every way without sin, see how we are attacked by our cruel enemies, the world, the flesh, and the devil. O Lord, come to our aid and deliver us from our enemies. Do not abandon us, O Lord, but purify our souls. O Lord who has compassion for our weakness, strengthen us, and grant us grace to fight beneath your banner and conquer our enemies. Amen.

For Kings and Authorities

Our great Father, King of kings, and Lord of lords, we appeal to you for the sake of all the faithful kings, rulers, our Emperor, the king and royal family of this land, and for all who exercise authority. Grant them wisdom, knowledge, a mind to do good, and your good counsel, and keep watch over them. Amen.

For All Humans

Before a meal

O Lord, we remember your compassion and the meal which you have given us to eat and drink by your grace. Make us worthy to praise and worship you through it. Amen.

When entering a church

For the glory of the Gospel

O Lord, who has promised that your spirit will be poured out over all flesh, have mercy upon all who do not know you. Take from them all foolishness, darkened faith, and hatred for your word. Help all Christians to show you to them by our actions and works. May the time of one shepherd and one flock come quickly. Amen.

For Those about to be ordained

Remember, O Lord, every bishop who tend to the holy Church throughout the world, and the whole heirarchy who govern us today, the Metropolitans, Elders, and Deacons. May you yourself choose worthy servants to do holy service in your Church. Govern their minds and fill them with the gift of your Holy Sprit, so that they may work tirelessly in your vineyard in whatever station they are called to.

The New Year

Our Lord, the Messiah, in whose hand are held the foundations of the earth and the heights of the mountains, who by your grace did arrange and bless the crown of the year and its months, may you bless this year's months, seasons, weeks, and days. Remove afflictions from us, and grant us long lasting joys. Bless our gardens, our fields, and the seeds we sow in them. Let this not be a year of fear and trembling and earthquakes. Our Lord, in the birth of this new year, may we commit our bodies souls and spirits into your hands. Gather us into your omnipotent governance, and be light and life in all our paths. Amen.

For Jews, Hindus, Muslims, etc.

O Lord, who has promised us blessedness above all blessedness, we praise you for your mercy in which you have called us and gathered us into the Kingdom of Grace which you established on earth. Yet, O Lord, look also at the millions upon millions of humans who still do not know you, and at the countless nations who walk in the darkness of their hearts and are consumed by abonimable idolatry. Have compassion on all Jews, Muslims, Hindus, Gentiles, and unbelievers, give them the light of your Gospel and thereby enlighten the eyes of their hearts so that they might know you and the wonders of your mercy. Pour out the gifts of faith and love over every Christian Church, and grant that we might magnify the Holy Name from morning till evening, and every day confess that Jesus Christ is Lord. Amen.

For evangelists and evangelical institutions

We bless you, O Lord, for your grace which proclaims your priceless Gospel in our midst and to the ends of the earth. Enlighten the minds and cleanse the hearts of all who proclaim your Gospel. Help them by your grace so that your kingdom might expand by their service. Prepare their paths, so that all who dwell in the darkness of ignorance might recieve them in joy, and give ear to your word. Bless every organization which had undertaken to spread your Gospel throughout the world. Send your Holy Spirit upon the preachers of the Gospel and those who hear it. Grant grace, so that we and all the members of the Church of God might work for the glory of your Kingdom, in faith and love, according to the grace given to each of us. Amen.

For the Sick

Omnipotent Lord God, the great physician who has made known his power in his word, by whose deeds displayed his activity, and who came, bearing medicines and aid, to heal our illness, heal this servant who takes refuge in your mercy and grace. Strengthen his/her body, and cast out illness. Wipe out his/her transgressions and raise him/her from sickness. Heal him/her from all affliction and punishment. Remit and abolish his/her sin and transgression so that he/she might thank you for all your mercies. Amen.

For the Harvest

O Lord, who by your divine might created all things, and supports all life, send your blessings unto our lands, fields, and estates, bless the yield therein, grant good seasons of sowing and reaping, and have mercy on us by the grace of your protection. Our Lord, grant that your word be planted in our hearts and that we might produce the fruitss of righteousness. Prepare our hearts for you, that we might recieve and partake in your every gift with a greatful heart.

For travellers

Lord God, who led your holy Apostles, and promised them "I will be with you until the end of the world", lead these your servant on their way to _____. Bring them back to us in peace and gladden us by their sight, that together we might glorify you, your Father, and your Holy Spirit, now and always and forever. Amen.

Before reading Holy Scripture

Lord God, grant us the knowledge of your divine words. Fill us with the essence of your holy scripture, the splendor of your divine grace, and the gifts of the Holy Spirit. Grant us grace now and at all times, that we might joyfully observe your commandments, fulfill your holy will, and become worthy of your blessings and mercies. Amen.

Sunday Prayers

Kudash Itha

Blessed art thou, O Lord, who built Your Church on the Prophets, mended it's flaws by the Apostles, adorned it in heavenly riches, and invited to it both natives and foreigners, May your eternal peace remain, O Lord, in your Church that you saved by your cross. Infuse Your love into it. Take away conflict, quarrels, and arguments from it, and make wise the Bishops and Priest to rightly shepherd and rule your sheep whom are saved by your blood. Amen.

Hudas Itha

Guard by your cross, O Lord, the Holy Church and her children. Send to dwell your Church your Holy Spirit who dwelt on the Prophets and Apostles and spoke through them. Give her Lambs food and drink, and seal the Children of the Church with the cross of light. Praise be to the unseen Father who chose the Church, the Son who saved her by His cross, and the Holy Spirit who sanctifies her.

Third Sunday

O Lord, who announced to Zachariah by your messenger Gabriel, "Your praye has been heard in the presence of God, and your wife Elizabeth shall bear a son in old age", may you look on us with your mercy, accept our prayer and declare for us too the annunciation of joy. Remove from Your Church heresies and unbelief. Make us worthy to make known your Gospel among the gentiles like your Messenger, and to magnify your holy name. Amen.

First Sunday of Great Lent

Gladden your Church, O Lord, who came with love to the wedding feast in the city of Cana, turned water into wine, gladdened the bridegroom and the guests, and revealed your power by a miracle. Pour out the wine of Your love, and delight us. Make us rejoice with the forgiveness of sins, and in the end, seat us at the spiritual table in the feast of your Kingdom. Amen.

Second Sunday of Great Lent

O Lord, who caused lepers to recieve healing by You, the blind to recieve sight at the sound of your voice, the crippled to become well, and demons to flee and hide from Your presence, may You, by Your grace, remove all public and secret diseases and pains from our bodies and souls, and satisfy us with your goodnesses. Amen.

Third Sunday of Great Lent

O Lord Messiah, who came down with mercy to look upon the sorrowful, to heal the sick, to drive out demons, and to free the oppressed, give us life by your holy word, for we are weakened by sin. May you not reject us because of our lack of faith, but Support and empower us with faith in You,
